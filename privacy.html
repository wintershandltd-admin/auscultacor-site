Privacy notice

How we handle information from people who visit this website or get in touch with us.

Last updated: date

Who we are

Ausculta Cor is a trading name of Winter Shand Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 17339504), with its registered office at 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, reference ZC206342.

For the information you give us through this website, Winter Shand Ltd is the controller. To contact us about your data, email hello@auscultacor.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, work email and company, plus anything else you choose to add (your role, website, contact volumes, message).
  • Booking a call: your name, email, the time you pick and your answers to the booking questions.
  • Email: your email address and what you write to us.
  • Visits: aggregate statistics about how the site is used, collected without cookies and without identifying you.

Why we use it

We use it to reply to you, to arrange and hold a discovery call, and to prepare a proposal if you ask for one. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us about our services, and, where you ask for a proposal, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that process it on our behalf:

  • Our form service: name, e.g. Formspree
  • Our booking tool: name, e.g. Cal.com
  • Our email provider: name
  • Our website host, GitHub (GitHub Pages), which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site
  • Our analytics provider: name, e.g. Plausible

International transfers

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where they do, the transfer relies on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. Confirm the safeguard for each provider

How long we keep it

If an enquiry doesn't lead to working together, we delete it after 24 months. If it does, we keep it for as long as the relationship lasts and as required for our accounts.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, and ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or stop using it. Email hello@auscultacor.com and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website doesn't set cookies. Our visit statistics are collected without them. The booking page opens on our booking tool's own site, which has its own privacy policy.

If your company becomes a client

Call recordings and transcripts we audit for a client are handled under a separate data processing agreement, where the client is the controller and Winter Shand Ltd is the processor. This notice doesn't cover that data.
