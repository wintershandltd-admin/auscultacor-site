How we audit

We've worked the queue we're auditing.

Localization vendors do language. AI QA tools score compliance. Neither has sat in a Spanish cancellation queue.

Our auditors came up through the Spanish customer service team of a global printing and marketing company, where one seat handled billing, subscriptions and angry customers, work that English teams split across several departments. Since then: renewal reminders, billing calls and gym membership collections. Customers who don't want to pay, feel trapped by a recurring charge, or are avoiding one.

We hear what that customer meant. A transcript can't.

The depth your auto-QA doesn't have

Automated QA scores every call, thinly. We go deep on 10 to 20 a month. That is a diagnostic sample, not coverage, and we say so up front.

Keep your auto-QA. Use us to find out what it misses.
