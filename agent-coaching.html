Agent Coaching · For audit clients

Fix the behavior the audit found.

A four-week program for your Spanish-language team, built from your own audited calls. For teams losing ground on cancellation pushback, payment objections and angry callers.

Start with a Retention AuditBook a discovery call

We only coach against problems we've found

Generic training teaches generic skills. Our coaching starts from a named problem in your own calls, such as six saveable cancellations lost at the same moment, and trains your team out of it. That's why it's only available after a Retention Audit.

What it covers

  • Holding a cancellation conversation without pushing or folding
  • Payment objections and billing disputes
  • De-escalating angry or anxious callers
  • Using the retention offers your policy allows, at the right moment
  • Hearing what the customer means beneath what they say

Four weeks

  1. Week 1

    Findings

    Walk the team through the audited moments and agree the target behaviors.

  2. Week 2

    Practice

    Role-plays built from your real calls.

  3. Week 3

    Pressure

    Harder scenarios with live feedback.

  4. Week 4

    Check

    Assessed calls and a handover plan for team leads.
