The renewal that became a chargeback

A customer finds a $479 annual renewal on his card. He is inside the refund window and says he wants a monthly plan. The agent defends the charge, warns him about suspension, and he hangs up to call his bank.

Reconstructed call. No real customer, no real company.

Company
CajaViva (invented), a US food-box subscription with Spanish-speaking customers
Plan
Annual, $479 (monthly plan: $49.99)
Agent could offer
Full refund within 14 days of renewal · Switch to monthly with the difference refunded · One free box as goodwill
Charged
6 days before the call
Reason code
Billing surprise
Recoverable
Yes

A refund on the call keeps a $49.99-a-month customer who said “me gustan las cajas.” A chargeback most likely loses the $479 anyway, adds a dispute fee and a mark on the merchant account, and loses the customer.

Where the call turned

The timestamped moments behind the scorecard.

What should have happened

The same moments, with wording your agents could use. Each offer is one the policy already allowed.

What to fix first

  • Check the refund window before answering any refund question.
  • Treat “banco”, “no autorizado” and “disputa” as a signal to resolve the call with an allowed option, never with consequences.
  • Never describe account penalties in reply to a dispute threat. Customers hear it as a threat.
  • Replace “usted aceptó los términos” with an acknowledgment of the surprise.
  • He says he never received the renewal reminder. Check that Spanish-speaking customers get it, in Spanish.

The call

Reconstructed call. No real customer, no real company. Written by our auditors from patterns they have handled for years. Personal details are removed, as they are in client reports.

Read the full transcript, in Spanish with English translation

Highlighted rows are the moments above.

  1. 00:00Agente

    CajaViva, buenas tardes, habla Brenda. ¿En qué le puedo servir?

    CajaViva, good afternoon, this is Brenda. How can I help you?

  2. 00:04Cliente

    [tono tenso] Sí, buenas. Me acaban de cobrar cuatrocientos setenta y nueve dólares en mi tarjeta y yo no autoricé eso.

    [tense] Yes, hi. I was just charged four hundred seventy-nine dollars on my card and I didn’t authorize that.

  3. 00:11Agente

    Permítame revisar. ¿Me da su número de pedido o su correo electrónico?

    Let me check. Can I have your order number or your email?

  4. 00:15Cliente

    [correo electrónico].

    [email address].

  5. 00:19Agente

    [tecleando] Muy bien, señor Ramírez. Veo que es la renovación automática de su plan anual.

    [typing] All right, Mr. Ramírez. I see it’s the automatic renewal of your annual plan.

  6. 00:25Cliente

    ¿Plan anual? Yo me inscribí hace un año con una promoción. Nadie me dijo que se iba a renovar solo, y menos por ese monto.

    Annual plan? I signed up a year ago with a promotion. Nobody told me it would renew on its own, much less for that amount.

  7. 00:33Agente

    Señor, la renovación automática está en los términos y condiciones que usted aceptó al registrarse.

    Sir, automatic renewal is in the terms and conditions you accepted when you signed up.

  8. 00:38Cliente

    [suspira fuerte] Mire, yo no leí ninguna letra pequeña. Yo pensé que era mes a mes. Ese dinero era para la renta.

    [heavy sigh] Look, I didn’t read any fine print. I thought it was month to month. That money was for rent.

  9. 00:45Agente

    Entiendo, pero el cargo es correcto. Además, se le envió un recordatorio por correo hace 30 días.

    I understand, but the charge is correct. Also, a reminder was emailed to you 30 days ago.

  10. 00:51Cliente

    Pues yo no recibí nada. [pausa] ¿Me lo pueden devolver o no?

    Well, I didn’t get anything. [pause] Can you refund it or not?

  11. 00:55Agente

    Lo que puedo hacer es cancelar la renovación para el próximo año, así ya no se le vuelve a cobrar.

    What I can do is cancel next year’s renewal, so you won’t be charged again.

  12. 01:01Cliente

    ¿El próximo año? Yo le estoy hablando de los cuatrocientos setenta y nueve de ahora.

    Next year? I’m talking about the four seventy-nine right now.

  13. 01:06Agente

    Las renovaciones anuales no son reembolsables, señor. Lo siento.

    Annual renewals are non-refundable, sir. I’m sorry.

  14. 01:10Cliente

    [levanta la voz] ¿No son reembolsables? Me cobran sin avisarme bien y ahora no me lo devuelven. Entonces voy a llamar a mi banco y lo reporto como un cargo no autorizado.

    [raises his voice] Non-refundable? You charge me without proper notice and now you won’t give it back. Then I’m going to call my bank and report it as an unauthorized charge.

  15. 01:19Agente

    Está en su derecho, señor. Pero le informo que si abre una disputa, su cuenta queda suspendida y pierde las cajas que le quedan del plan.

    That’s your right, sir. But I should tell you that if you open a dispute, your account will be suspended and you’ll lose the remaining boxes on your plan.

  16. 01:27Cliente

    ¿Me está amenazando?

    Are you threatening me?

  17. 01:29Agente

    No, señor, solo le explico el procedimiento.

    No, sir, I’m just explaining the procedure.

  18. 01:33Cliente

    [pausa larga] Mire, yo solo quería seguir con el plan de mes a mes. Me gustan las cajas. Pero no así.

    [long pause] Look, I just wanted to stay on the month-to-month plan. I like the boxes. But not like this.

  19. 01:40Agente

    Entiendo. ¿Desea que cancele la renovación automática entonces?

    I understand. Would you like me to cancel the automatic renewal, then?

  20. 01:44Cliente

    [tono cortante] Haga lo que quiera. Yo ya voy a llamar al banco.

    [curt] Do whatever you want. I’m calling the bank.

  21. 01:48Agente

    Muy bien. Queda cancelada la renovación para el próximo periodo. ¿Algo más en lo que le pueda ayudar?

    Very well. The renewal for the next period is cancelled. Anything else I can help you with?

  22. 01:53Cliente

    No.

    No.

  23. 01:54

    [El cliente cuelga.]

    [The customer hangs up.]
