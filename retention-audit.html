How a month runs

  1. You share 10–20 recent interactions.

    Spanish-language cancellation, billing and retention calls or chats, sent securely under our data processing agreement. Recordings work best: tone is half the finding.

  2. We judge each one against a written standard.

    Every recoverable judgment is checked against our written standard, and borderline calls get a second reviewer.

  3. You get the report and a walkthrough call.

    Four parts, same shape every month, so you can track movement. A 30-minute call walks you through the findings.

  4. Next month, we measure again.

    The second audit shows whether the changes landed.

We only count the saves we're sure of

Every call gets one judgment: was it recoverable? Our definition is strict and written down.

So our number undercounts, on purpose. You can take it to finance without having to defend it.

A call is recoverable only if the customer voiced a specific, addressable objection, and the agent had a lever your policy allows that they didn't use.

Anything ambiguous doesn't count.

Done, missed or mishandled

No 1–5 scales. In each of the five areas we listen for, the call is marked done, missed or mishandled. Scales start arguments about numbers. Plain findings get acted on.

When cancelling is made too hard

If we hear an agent making it harder to cancel than it should be, we flag it. We report it as an operational observation for your legal or compliance team to evaluate. We don't give legal advice.

Questions we get asked

Why only 10 to 20 interactions?

It's a deep diagnostic sample, not coverage. Each one gets an experienced listener and a written judgment, and borderline calls get a second reviewer. Need a bigger sample? We'll quote it.

We already use AI QA. Is this a replacement?

No. Auto-QA scores everything thinly. We go deep on a few and find what a scorecard can't hear. They work together.

Recordings or transcripts?

Recordings where you can. Hesitation, tone and being talked over don't survive a transcript. Chat logs work as they are.

What language is the report in?

English, with Spanish quotes translated alongside.

How do you handle our customer data?

We act as your data processor under a written agreement. Only named auditors bound by the same terms hear your calls, and we delete recordings on the schedule you set.

What does it cost?

It depends on your volumes and how many interactions you want audited. We quote after a short discovery call.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes, three months to start. The second and third audits are what show the changes are working.
